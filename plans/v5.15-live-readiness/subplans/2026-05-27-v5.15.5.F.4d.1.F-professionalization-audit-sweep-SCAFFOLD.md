## Why this ship exists

Post-`.B.6` codebase-wide audit sweep (2026-05-27) surfaced concrete evidence that **AI-driven coding INCREASES audit value, does not decrease it.** Specifically, the sweep caught 3 distinct AI-introduced bug classes in ONE session:

1. **Class 26 ×2** at Async.hpp:814+853 — AI mechanical migration substituted wrong index variable (ea08210; silent trading-logic miscalibration)
2. **Class 14 fabricated symbols** in `.B.7` plan body draft — AI invented panel names (Replay, etc.) that don't exist
3. **Class 33 consumer-enumeration-undercount** at `.B.6` close — AI undercounted TECH_DEBT-131 scope despite codified discipline

Pattern: AI hallucinates, substitutes mechanically without scope verification, undercounts consumers, doesn't "feel" code rot. Audits FIND these bugs; AI APPLIES the fixes. Symbiosis. Without `.E` sweep, paper-test or live surfaces these silent bugs at high cost.

`.E` placement (between `.D` and v5.15 sprint umbrella close) is the natural sprint-close gate. `.B.7`+`.C`+`.D` are framework-consolidation WORK; `.E` is verification WORK. Running `.E` AFTER framework-lock ensures audits don't catch transient `.C`/`.D` state.

This ship was PROPOSED by Caramel 2026-05-27 in response to "should we check other areas of code that need professionalization? or run like data flow checks and stuff to identify or more issues? what plans remain for closing out 5.15.F" + the followup "unless this reduces risk with ai as well" — confirmed by evidence; ship is GO.

## Design space + future-oriented choices

### Decision A — Parallel fire vs sequential fire

| Option | Description | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **(a) Parallel fire (multi-agent)** | All audits fire concurrently via general-purpose subagents | **CHOSEN** — proven at post-.B.6 sweep; ~30-45 min wall-time for full sweep |
| (b) Sequential fire | Run audits one-by-one in main session | rejected — wall-time prohibitive; main-session context bloat |
| (c) Tiered fire (HIGH first; MED conditional on HIGH findings) | Conditional cascade | rejected for simplicity; if HIGH surfaces blocking findings, operator can cancel MED tier inline |

### Decision B — Triage shape (fold-into-.E vs queue-as-TECH_DEBT)

Per `feedback_proportionate_response_to_audit_findings`:

| Finding severity | Default action | Override conditions |
|---|---|---|
| CRITICAL (silent production-path bug) | FIX-NOW (fold into .E) | Operator override → emergency hotfix interrupt |
| HIGH (drift surface; AI-introduced risk) | FIX-NOW (fold into .E) IF small + bounded; ELSE TECH_DEBT | Operator decides per-finding |
| MED (optimization opportunity; future-proofing) | TECH_DEBT (queue for v5.16 sprint or .E follow-up) | Fold if low-cost + high-ROI |
| LOW (cosmetic; nit) | TECH_DEBT or acknowledge-as-acceptable | Triage at operator discretion |

### Decision C — Scope split-vs-folded if findings volume too large

If `.E` findings volume > ~10 HIGH or > ~50 total → consider spawning v5.16-professionalization-audit-followup sub-sprint for systemic close-out. `.E` ship itself focuses on CRITICAL + HIGH fix-now; everything else moves to v5.16 backlog.
