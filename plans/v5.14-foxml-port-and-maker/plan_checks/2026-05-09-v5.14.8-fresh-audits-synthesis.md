## HIGH gaps

### F5. Three-axis asymmetric naming (all 4 audits)
ModelStampResult / StampInferenceCfgInputs / ModelHandle use 3 different naming conventions. ModelHandle's prefix policy is INCONSISTENT across groups (`stamp_inf_` / `stamp_xgb_` / `stamp_label_` / `stamp_` / no-prefix).

**Resolution chosen:** Option 1 (full unification). Rename all 3 structs to canonical wire keys. ~14 files mechanical migration. Eliminates asymmetry permanently. Closes TECH_DEBT-006 entirely.

### F6. ModelHandle is partial mirror (all audits)
Not all registry fields exist on ModelHandle (e.g., `held_out_fraction`, `inference_cfg_drift_count`). **Resolution:** Add per-struct PRESENCE column to registry tuple (default ALL_THREE; overrides for parser-only fields).

### F7. Stranded WIP macro (3 audits)
Uncommitted `FOREACH_STAMP_BOUND_MODEL_CONST_STANDALONE` macro at lines 160-166 of StampBoundModelConstRegistry.hpp; doc text references it. **Resolution:** Land in pre-flight ship.

### F8. FOREACH_FEATURE has 7 caller sites, not 6 (trace-deps + readiness)
Plan claims line numbers ~507, 515, 521, 527, 562, 626, 693; current FeatureRegistry.hpp differs. **Resolution:** Re-enumerate at v5.14.8.E coding time.

### F9. xgb_tree_method (char[16]) handling incomplete (parity-check)
Registry has `STAMP_MODEL_CONST_PARSE_int/uint64_t/double` but no `_char_array` parser. **Resolution:** Type-driven `if constexpr` dispatch in pre-flight ship.
