### Gotchas this session paid for (read before repeating)

- **A background wrapper's exit code is not your build's.** `cmd > log; RC=$?; echo ...` exits with the *echo*. The notification said "exit code 0" while the compile had failed, and I ran a stale binary and read its 3732 as green — the count not moving is what caught it. Structure background commands to `exit $RC`.
- **clangd diagnostics on this tree are unreliable** — headers parsed standalone produce "no template named NodeContext" and false ambiguity. The build is the oracle. (But *one* of those clangd ambiguity warnings was real: `MASK_DRIFT_KILL_TRIPPED` exists at global scope AND in `tt::` from a different registry. Live TECH_DEBT-092 instance; qualify with `::`.)
- **You cannot demonstrate UB in a test.** A control that constructs an uninitialized struct and expects the poison to survive fails at -O2 while the bug is entirely real. Assert the compile-time property instead.
