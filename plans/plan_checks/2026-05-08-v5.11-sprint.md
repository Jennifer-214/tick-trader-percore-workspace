## Cross-Plan Integration Findings

### A. CRITICAL — v5.10.0e Drift Detection Branching vs v5.11 Invariants [ACTION REQUIRED]

**Gap:** v5.10.0e ships immediately before v5.11. If `auto_kill_on_drift` is implemented in 5.10.0e using a standard `if (drift)` branch on the hot path, it will violate the "Branchless Execution" invariant that v5.11 introduces immediately after.
**Recommendation:** Amend the `v5.10.0e-drift-detection.md` plan to explicitly adopt the HFT Sidecar Item 3: use a bitwise atomic mask inside a cache-aligned control block, avoiding hot-path branches from day one.

### B. GAP — Scheduling `strcmp` Refactor & Feature Mask [ACTION REQUIRED]

**Gap:** The HFT Sidecar elevated the `strcmp` refactor (jump tables) and Idea 5 (AVX-512 Feature Masks) from "style/deferred" to "HFT Invariants". However, they are still living in `v5.11-deferred-items.md` and are not scheduled into the 9 ships of the 5.11 Master Plan.
**Recommendation:** Fold the `strcmp` jump table refactor into `v5.11.0` (System foundation) or `v5.11.4` (Parsing SIMD). Fold the AVX-512 Feature Mask into `v5.11.2` (Slow path vectorization) or leave it deferred but explicitly marked as requiring AVX-512 when picked up.

### C. VERIFIED — SIMD JSON Reconcile Alignment [CLEAN]

**Finding:** The Sidecar identified the O(N*K) penalty in `Reconcile.hpp`. The master plan correctly schedules this exact fix (Part 10.2) into `v5.11.4 — Parsing SIMD` alongside the WebSocket `ud_parse_execution_report` optimization. The plans are perfectly aligned here.

### D. GAP — Rollback Testing Orchestration [ACTION REQUIRED]

**Gap:** `v5.11-deferred-items.md` flagged a missing "Rollback verification" step (checking out `pre-tag` and running tests). Given that `v5.11.1` and `v5.11.2` explicitly modify the extremely sensitive hot/slow paths, rollback safety is paramount.
**Recommendation:** Add the `git checkout pre-v5.11.X && ./build.sh test` check to the sprint's standard `/ship` protocol before starting `v5.11.1`.
