## What landed

1. **Uninitialized stack → `tp_pct`/`sl_pct`.** `MASK_EZOO_BANDITS_READY` means "the init pass ran",
   not "bandits are usable": `InitBandits` sets it and returns early WITHOUT `Bandit_Init` when
   `n_arms < 2`. `Bandit_GetProbabilities` then writes NOTHING (both loops are `i < n_arms == 0`), so
   `weights_buf` reached the barrier blend uninitialized. Fixed by guarding select on
   `primary_count >= 2` — the same test `InitBandits` itself uses. Not sanitizer-catchable: that
   function carries `no_sanitize("address")`, and ASan does not detect uninitialized STACK anyway.
