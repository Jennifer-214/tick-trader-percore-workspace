## Theme

Sprint goal: make "engine runs" structurally indistinguishable from
"engine ships." Per-tick safety alone isn't production-readiness; the
boot path + ship-time + hot-swap + drift surfaces all need
defense-in-depth before flipping to live trading.

The sprint cleared 6 TECH_DEBT items + 4 PARITY items + 1 known landmine
across structural refactors (X-macro migrations, helper extractions,
shadow-load) rather than direct patches. Per Caramel's "structural fix
> direct patch" direction (CLAUDE.md item 19), every recurring-bug-class
risk in the sprint was closed at the architectural level, not patched
at the symptom level.

## What surprised me across the sprint

### 1. Audit-driven pre-coding caught a fatal v5.15.4 design flaw

PARITY-023 surfaced during pre-coding `/parity-check`: the originally-
planned HotSwapSnapshot/Revert design had captured pointers to
soon-to-be-freed memory. Audit caught it before any code was written.
Plan amended to shadow-load (which is structurally correct: no torn
moment, no revert needed). The fatal flaw was never shipped to
production. This validates the audit-driven pre-coding gate
(`DESIGN_SPECS/audit-driven-pre-coding-gate.md`) — pre-coding audits
on MEDIUM+ ships have caught real architectural flaws before
implementation in 2 of the last 5 sub-ships.

### 2. Class 18 mirror class is structurally extinguished

PARITY-002 through -005 + -008 (4 historical recurrences) + PARITY-020
(this sprint) — all "same production-caller pattern at multiple sites
drifted apart" bugs — are now extinguished by 2 structural patterns:
- AUTOPOPULATE companion macro (CLAUDE.md item 21; closed PARITY-002/3/4/5/8)
- Stamp_AssembleAndEmit orchestration helper (this sprint; closed PARITY-020)

No production caller can fall out of sync; future stamp-emit callers
inherit AUTOPOPULATE + per-call population via the helper automatically.

### 3. Class trajectory matches mature-codebase signature

PARITY/TECH_DEBT items this sprint are LESS severe than earlier ones:
- PARITY-020/021: 2nd-line-of-defense gaps (drift detection); not
  1st-line breaks
- PARITY-022: precautionary quarantine (zero production callers
  pre-quarantine)
- PARITY-023: pre-coding catch (never shipped to production)
- TECH_DEBT-005/024/028: all closed cleanly via structural fixes
- NEW TECH_DEBT-034/035/036/037: deferred infrastructure work; no
  live bugs

This is what a maturing codebase looks like: recurring-bug classes
structurally closed, remaining items are observability gaps + deferred
infra. Per Caramel's analysis ("are the tech debt items were finding
major or way less severe than earlier ones") — yes, way less severe.

### 4. Boot-path heap migration was implicit in shadow-load

v5.15.4 plan didn't enumerate the static→heap migration of
`ml_zoos[]` + `ml_ensemble_zoos[]` at engine boot. But it's
inseparable from shadow-load's `free(old_ezoo)` safety. ~30 LOC
substitution; implemented inline rather than splitting to a sub-ship
per `feedback_no_defer_for_effort`.

### 5. Sprint operated under "structural fix > direct patch" direction

Caramel invoked the rule directly during v5.15.3 ("we should do A right,
like its the full fix isnt it? and is a structural fix rather than
apatch?") and v5.15.4 ("descoping it goes against more work now for
less in the future right? we should address it structurally following
design principles"). Both times the structural fix was the right call;
both times effort estimates were on the lower end of expectations
(scope was right-sized, not inflated).

## Paper-test handoff items

Before flipping `trading_mode=live` in any production deploy:

1. **Run paper-test cycle** with `trading_mode=paper` (default) for at
   least 1 week to validate v5.15 didn't regress paper PnL.
2. **Run shadow-mode** for at least 1 week to validate ML stamps load
   cleanly + drift detection works on real-time data flow.
3. **Promote shadow-load DESIGN_SPECS to CLAUDE.md item 28** once
   paper-test confirms shadow-load works under real cycle pressure
   (currently DRAFT-pending-promotion).
4. **`/parity-check` post-v5.15 final-audit pass** to verify HMAC
   chain byte-equivalence on real models trained pre-v5.14 + loaded
   post-v5.15. Surface G forward-compat should make this clean but
   verify on real artifacts.
5. **Operator deploy checklist** — when flipping to live, expect
   stderr to log `[live_normalize] trading_mode=live: model_verify_strict
   0→1 (STRICT)` + same for reconcile_mode. Operator confirms intent
   OR sets either explicitly to override.
