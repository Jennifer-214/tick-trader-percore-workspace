### Decision 1 — Drop Option A++ scope-creep (named-cluster sub-struct)

**What was decided:** The original Option A++ "grow to 6-field named cluster `OmsPerSlotContext` sub-struct + alignas(64) + extract registry primitive" was wrong because:
- `FOREACH_OMS_PER_SLOT_FIELD` already exists at `MemHeaders/OmsFieldRegistry.hpp:321` (since v5.15.5.C.5; 3 entries; 4-arg tuple `X(NAME[_i], TYPE, INIT, RESET)`)
- Named-cluster sub-struct is cosmetic — doesn't physically rearrange flat sibling SoA arrays
- Mixed Pattern 4 framework concerns with cache layout concerns (cache layout belongs in HP_REFACTOR.md scope)

**Replacement:** Extend existing `FOREACH_OMS_PER_SLOT_FIELD` by 2 rows — `last_exit_fee[_i]` (closes latent drift from .F.4c.3 WIP2d-1.B.1) + `bandit_reward_bps[_i]` (new for .F.4c.4). 4-arg tuple shape preserved. No sub-struct. No physical relayout.

**Why this matters for resumption:** plan body § N.2 + Step 7 (lines 1009-1014 region) STILL describes the old Option A++ shape. Must rewrite to reflect "extend existing registry by 2 rows" instead. See task #23 description.
