## Why this ship exists

Ship A (D-125/D-130) flipped `FPN<64>` to the 16B two's-complement core but **deliberately did NOT rename it**: D-143 deferred the cosmetic `FPN`→`FPN_Binary` rename to a dedicated ship so the ~2,144 consumer sites rode the flip unchanged and Ship A's diff showed ONLY the representation change (the D-125 diff-anchor discipline). D-151 then refined the riding mechanism from alias to full-specialization (`FixedPoint/FixedPointN.hpp:39-40,104-105`).

A.5 pays that deferral now, against the stable 16B anchor, BEFORE Ship B — because Ship B introduces `FPN_Decimal` (`FixedPoint<10,8>`) and migrates money onto it. If the binary type still spells bare `FPN` when decimal lands, the naming is ambiguous exactly where the binary/decimal domain split (B6 disjoint traits; future H4 "decimal-money / binary-features") most needs to be loud. A rename bundled into B would bury B's money diff (same reason it was kept out of A).

Secondary purpose: **A.5 is the tooling rehearsal for `.E.1`'s Core→Node rename** (~5,000+ sites, high ambiguity). A.5 proves the rename-ship pipeline on a low-ambiguity token first and codifies it as `rename-ship-methodology.md` (Stage-2 DRAFT at Step 0; `.E.1` = 2nd application). Cross-links TECH_DEBT-142 (HIGH — NOT closed here; closes at `.E.1`).
