### Item 1 — `CoreFrameworks/DerivedFilterFramework.hpp` (NEW)

3 macro variants per `metadata-bit-driven-derived-filter-framework.md` Stage 3 ACTIVE:

- `DERIVED_FILTER_DECLARE_GUI(NAME, SOURCE_FOREACH, METADATA_BIT)` — runtime walk over descriptor array; per-row callback fn-pointer invocation. For GUI-only filters (no HMAC chain).
- `DERIVED_FILTER_DECLARE_WIRE_FORMAT(NAME, SOURCE_FOREACH, METADATA_BIT, LOCKED_HASH_VAR, FIXTURE_PATH)` — extends GUI variant with Layer 5b hash-lock scaffold (synthetic populate fn + `fnv1a_64` canonical body hash + LOCKED const).
- `DERIVED_FILTER_DECLARE_WIRE_FORMAT_TWO_SOURCE(NAME, SOURCE_FOREACH, METADATA_BIT, BITMAP_SOURCE, BITMAP_FIELD, LOCKED_HASH_VAR, FIXTURE_PATH)` — extends WIRE_FORMAT with bitmap-source walking for fields living in sister `FOREACH_*_CFG_FLAG` bitmap registry.

**Estimate:** ~2-3h.

| Default | `STAMP_HAS(*h, inference_cfg)` |

**Winsor cohort cfg parse-time validation (~10 LOC at ControllerConfig.hpp parser):** enforces `winsor_pct_low > 0 && winsor_pct_high < 1 && winsor_pct_low < winsor_pct_high` at boot; moves compound predicate from emit-time to cfg parse boundary.
