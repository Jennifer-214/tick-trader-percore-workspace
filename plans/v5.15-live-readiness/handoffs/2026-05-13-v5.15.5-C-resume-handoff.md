## One last thing — does this make maintenance easier?

**Yes.** Concretely:

1. **OrderManagerState HOT/WARM/COLD reorg** (.C.1) — adding a new
   field now has a CLEAR home (hot path, warm path, or cold path).
   No more "where do I put this field?" friction. Field-add latency
   impact is bounded to one tier; cross-thread atomics are isolated
   in their own cache lines so the next contributor can add an
   observability counter without worrying about false sharing.

2. **Order<F> internal reorg** + size-invariant static_asserts — any
   future field addition trips the static_assert if it changes
   sizeof(Order<64>); contributor must consciously choose to update
   the assertion (with a written justification) instead of silently
   bloating OrderPool slot footprint.

3. **NC1 + NC2 DESIGN_SPECS** — when a future cache-layout sweep on
   ANOTHER struct hits RAII destructor or SPSC-ring placement
   questions, the answers are pre-canonicalized. No reinvention.

   cohorts close out the remaining Class-18 mirror sites in OMS;
   adding the next OMS field becomes 1 row in `FOREACH_OMS_INIT`.

   crashes on fresh checkouts without .env.* files. One less friction
