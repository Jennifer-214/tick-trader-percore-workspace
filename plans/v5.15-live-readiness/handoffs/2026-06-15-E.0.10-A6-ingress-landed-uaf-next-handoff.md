### A6 INGRESS — engine `3ee95dc` (14 files)

The design (operator-decided, gate-validated; D-221 in the decision log carries the full reshape + the trace verdict "CLEARED to build"):

**Per-arm detect → per-arm drop → majority → node-SHALT.** A corrupt barrier (`tp_pct`/`sl_pct` that is NaN / +Inf / negative / over a sane cap) is caught at model-load; the poisoned ensemble arm is masked off; if a **majority** of the arms are corrupt (configurable ratio, default 0.5), the whole node SHALTs (refuse-to-trade) rather than trade on a mostly-poisoned blend. Caramel's explicit refinements are all in: **MAJORITY threshold** (not a fixed 66% — she caught that 5 models never hit 66%), **block-new-only**, **SANE_MAX as a percentage** (equity-agnostic; separate SL/TP caps), **per-arm corrupt visibility**, the **trainer-emit floor folded in** (adjacency by structure+function, not file-location), and **fully-disable corrupt arms** ("rather retrain than have silent/bad models trading").
