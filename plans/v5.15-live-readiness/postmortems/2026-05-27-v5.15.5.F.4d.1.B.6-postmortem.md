Operator caught it; ~30 min of agent work reverted. Lesson codified in file-size-split-discipline.md v1.3 + sister memory `feedback_count_code_loc_not_total_lines`. Going-forward: COMPUTE CODE-LOC BEFORE TRIGGERING SPLIT WORK.

2. **Phase B.5 (test_common.hpp) was scope-pivoted mid-cycle** — Originally planned as full controller_test.cpp domain split; operator pivot ("im really not too picky about this phase; more concerned about actual code") downscoped to test_common.hpp helper extract + TECH_DEBT-127 queued for full split. Then folded into .B.6 ship informally (no separate .B.5 tag). Slight version-progression awkwardness; operator framing was "we'll rename to 5.16 eventually anyway".

3. **Agent's B.4 commit miscounted SH_* macro location** — Plan body Decision G said "SH_* macros + DumpLatency are atomic extraction unit". Agent's read found SH_* macros actually live inside EngineSharded_Run's TUI render block (not around DumpLatency). Decision G framing was technically inaccurate but the atomic-unit principle still held (don't try to hoist SH_* separately). Minor doc-vs-reality drift; corrected in Run.hpp extract by moving entire EngineSharded_Run body as one unit.
