## .B.8 — ShardedSnapshot 4-walk → 1-walk consolidation

**Goal:** Memory bandwidth efficiency. Today's `ShardedSnapshot.hpp` walks `state->cores[i]` in 4 separate loops (lines 297, 347, 412, 429). Each loop pulls full CoreContext into L2 (~1KB × 16 cores × 3 redundant pulls ≈ 48KB/pub × 60Hz = 46MB/s memory bandwidth waste). Cluster boundaries from .B.1 + .B.4 enable cache-warm single-pass traversal.

**LOC:** ~80.
