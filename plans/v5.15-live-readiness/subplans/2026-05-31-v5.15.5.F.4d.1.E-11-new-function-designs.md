## #1 — unified `FixedPoint<RADIX,FRAC>` type [LOCKED — D-125/126]

## #2 — the shared multiply [FRAMED]
`a × b` = **[extract sign + abs] → [unsigned 128×128→256 product — HOISTED certified body] → [reduce: RADIX-FORK] → [reapply sign]**.
- The certified **unsigned** product (FP64_Mul's guts) is reused **unchanged** (C1=A HOIST). Two's-complement operands are abs'd at entry (−2⁶³ guard), sign = `(A<0)^(B<0)` reapplied after the reduce → **value-equivalent to sign-magnitude BY CONSTRUCTION** (same unsigned product, same sign rule) — exactly what the D-125 net needs.
- Two's-complement nets a win: add/sub/compare SIMPLER (native), mul/div NEUTRAL (abs-in/out, same magnitude work), storage SMALLER (16B).
- The widen (256-bit product) is a **hard math constraint at 128-bit** (the exact `A·B` needs 2× the bits before ÷SCALE; can't skip for exact/deterministic). NOT a latency bottleneck (4 native partials, slow-path cadence, ~6ns vs the ≤100µs budget). Dissolving it = the 8B future ship (operands fit → native `__int128` product). Decimal's operands are small (≤63-bit) so its product is effectively native already; the genuine manual 256-bit widen is binary's alone.
- **Fork lives ONLY in the reduce** (everything before is shared + sign-agnostic).
- OPEN: pin against `FP64_Mul`'s exact body at framing→code.

## #3 — `divmul_pow10` (decimal reduce) [FRAMED]
Decimal reduce `C = P / 10⁸` via **Granlund–Montgomery reciprocal-multiply**: `q = (P × M) >> S`, **constexpr M/S** (10⁸ is compile-time). Replaces `__udivti3` (~40–100 *variable* cyc) with a fixed-cost multiply+shift (~20 cyc) that **REUSES #2's wide-multiply primitive** (same 256-bit product, shifted to grab the high end). Returns **(quotient q, remainder r = P − q·10⁸)** — `r` feeds #4.
- Mechanism: turns expensive arbitrary-÷ into cheap `×M` + **free power-of-2 shift** (`÷2ˢ`). **EXACT** (not approximate) — bit-identical `floor(P/10⁸)`, *iff the magic is proven*.
- **CORRECTNESS CRUX:** prove `(M,S)` exact over the full dividend range (G–M bound; enumerate-not-eyeball per `enumerate_set_before_categorical_claim`); validate vs the D-100 external decimal oracle. A wrong magic = silently-wrong money.
- Binary reduce, for contrast: `P >> 64` (free shift, no magic) → `divmul_pow10` is **decimal-only**.
- Rejected: factoring `10⁸=2⁸·5⁸` (truncating `P>>8` first corrupts the round).
- **GENERAL PRIMITIVE:** divide-by-invariant-constant for *wide* types → DESIGN_SPEC candidate (task #14) composing `branchless-math-kernel-pattern`; also the only way to SIMD-divide (AVX has no integer divide).

## #4 — the rounding helper [FRAMED — mode DECIDED D-128]
**Mode decided (D-128):** round-**HALF-EVEN (banker's)** for internal reduces; **venue-matched** where the venue defines it; **uniform EVERYWHERE incl. replay** (D-105). Branchless, from divmul_pow10's `(q, r)`, `0 ≤ r < SCALE`:
- **Half-even (banker's — internal default):**
  `round_up = (2r > SCALE) | ((2r == SCALE) & (q & 1));  result_mag = q + round_up`
  — `2r < 2·SCALE` (~28 bits, no overflow). The `(q & 1)` is the round-to-**EVEN** tie-break: an exact half rounds up only when `q` is odd (→ even). Unbiased over many fills.
- **Round-up (venue fee, D-109):** `round_up = (r != 0)` (= ceil at precision).
- **Truncate:** `round_up = 0` (binary doesn't use this helper — it shift-reduces; decimal-only).
- Operates on the **unsigned** `(q,r)` magnitude from #3; sign reapplied after (from #2). **Single source** → uniform incl. replay (D-105) by construction.

**D-93 FOLD (2026-06-09, D-174 — full audit: `d93-design-audit-rounding-div` + `d93-design-audit-parse-casts`):**
- **Divisor-generalized, overflow-free tie form (REPLACES the `2r` compare):** `2r` overflows for #7's runtime divisors (signed UB at r ≥ 2¹²⁶). One body serves ALL divisors — `half = d >> 1; round_up = (r > half) | ((r == half) & ((d & 1) == 0) & (q & 1))` — unsigned 128-bit end-to-end; ties exist only for even d; d = SCALE (mul-reduce) / 2⁶⁴ (to_decimal — bit-test fast-path allowed) / runtime divisor (#7). This IS D-105 single-source by construction: one rounding body, divisor-parameterized.
- **Ceil variant pinned (D-174d):** NONNEG-domain only (magnitude-ceil ≠ ROUND_CEILING on negatives — the oracle disagreement caught at the audit) + confined INSIDE `Money_FeeCompute` — NO public rounding-mode parameter anywhere.
- **Oracle additions (P1 deliverable):** constructed NEGATIVE ties (random pairs hit ties with p≈0.003 — they were never tested); `oracle_fee_roundup` actually CALLED (it was defined-but-never-invoked — the prior "oracle-verified" claim was partially false); #7 Div rows; cast rows (ties + saturate boundary).
