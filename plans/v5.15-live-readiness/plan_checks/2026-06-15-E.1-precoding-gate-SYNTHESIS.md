# `.E.1` Foundation — pre-coding gate cycle-1 synthesis

## Combined verdict: **YELLOW-leaning-RED**

The **architecture is sound and DAG-validated** — the cluster/node hierarchy, the push-model O(1) aggregator, the drainer-absorption-into-per-node-slow-path, the FOREACH_EXCHANGE substrate, the canonical-sister-extension discipline. **No agent argued against the design.** Every blocking finding is *plan-currency* + *fold-design* + *scope*, not architectural error.

But the plan body is **v0.1 (2026-05-28), drafted before the entire numeric-core rework (Ships A/A.5/B) AND before `.E.0.1`–`.E.0.10`** — and it bundles 7 enormous axes into one ship. It is **not ready to code**; it needs re-grounding + fold-design + (recommended) decomposition first.

## Why only 2 of 7 agents (proportionality)

The 2 SHAPE/SCOPE agents converged decisively on "re-ground + decompose + design-the-folds." The 5 deep domain lenses (dod / accounting / parity / hft / blindspot) audit **implementation detail** — their value is against *real* `Money` types + *designed* folds + *refreshed* anchors. Firing them on stale `FPN<F>` sketches about to be re-typed + decomposed is ~1M tokens largely re-confirming the known re-grounding. They fire with far more signal **per re-grounded sub-plan**. (`feedback_proportionate_response_to_audit_findings` + `feedback_planning_treadmill_bias_to_execution`.)
