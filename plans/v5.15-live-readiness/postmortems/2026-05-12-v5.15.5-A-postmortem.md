## Theme

Close the train-serve barrier-parity gap. Multi-horizon models trained against
label-specific barriers were being served against single `cfg.ml_tp_pct` /
`cfg.ml_sl_pct` — operator-set values that may have diverged from training-time
values. Tier 1 cfg-drift promotion + per-horizon barrier dispatch + structural
unification of the entire drift-detection surface.

Required prerequisite for credible multi-horizon ensemble support in production
(Caramel 2026-05-12: "required for multi-horizon/multi-model support").

## What surprised me during .A.7

### 1. TECH_DEBT-037 discovered mid-coding

Halfway through writing `CfgDriftCheckRegistry.hpp` walker, surfaced that the
4 new entries (originally appended to FOREACH_STAMP_BOUND_CFG) would NOT
auto-generate ModelHandle fields — that's MODEL_CONST registry territory.
Caught before any broken commit; recovery was bounded.

Pivoted to the structural close instead of a bounded recovery (Caramel:
"what is the structural fix that completely closes tech debt items, and
make it easy to extend in the future and uses the data oriented design
principles?"). The structural close added ~80 LOC over the bounded Option A
but extinguished the manual-section-2a class permanently.

### 2. Dual-axis Y3 dispatch is a generalized pattern

The 3-axis dispatch (severity × category × compare_kind) in FOREACH_CFG_DRIFT_CHECK
turned out to be a clean GENERALIZATION of the single-axis Y3 precedents
(emit_source in StampBoundCfg, storage_class in FailureMode, scope in
SlowPathGate). Codified as `dual-axis-y3-dispatch-pattern.md` (DESIGN_SPEC
DRAFT v0.1 pending field validation at this ship's close).

### 3. Per-category fail_mask vs per-entry — uint16_t headroom forces the choice

`drift_flags_at_load` is uint16_t with 9/16 BIT_FLAGs used pre-.A.7. Per-entry
fail_mask would have needed 18 new bits → overflow. Per-CATEGORY (2 bits added
for cfg_binding_drift reuse + new cfg_cross_binary_drift) fits cleanly with
11/16 used post-.A.7. Per-entry granularity is a future option if failure_flags
widens to uint32_t.

### 4. Log injection via template-deferred-dependency-injection

3rd application of the pattern after v5.14.4.B Reconcile_ApplyMissedFills /
AutoCancelStale. Default template arg = `tt::StderrLog` makes the migration
boundary-stable for production callers (no caller signature changes). Tests
use C++17 generic lambdas (local classes can't have template member functions;
generic lambda with `auto...` packs IS allowed inside main).
