unversioned, unguarded, hand-maintained. With N consumers and M facts, restatement gives N×M drift

instance count is a function of surface count, which is what we keep adding. It is the doc-plane
instance of the problem **H15–H19** close on the engine plane (auto-flowing registry > manual N-site
sync). Shipping `1.0.0` with prose facts unbound means versioning the drift generator into the
