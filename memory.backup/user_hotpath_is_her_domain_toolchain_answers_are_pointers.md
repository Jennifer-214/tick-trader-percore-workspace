Caramel's domain expertise is **hot-path / engine architecture** — the OMS, the per-node sharding, the money paths. On **toolchain / dev-environment** surfaces (more algorithmic, less hers) she has deliberately deferred the micro-architecture to me, and her answers there are correspondingly vaguer.

**Read a vague toolchain answer as a POINTER, not a gap.** It means *"we decided this somewhere — go find it"*, not *"I don't know"*. Her words 2026-07-20: *"i dont really remember what i decided on for alot of this stuff, but we have decision logs and a north star for the toolchain v1 finish line, so we should constantly keep that stuff in mind"* and *"my expertise is within the actual hotpath architecture, not toolchain environments where its more algorithmic heavy"*.

**Why:** the workspace repo IS the externalized memory for this half of the work (`user_structure_is_correctness_risk_control_for_capital`). Asking her to recall a toolchain decision re-derives by hand what the corpus already records — and her recall is the *weaker* source there, so a confident-sounding answer from memory is the risk, not the help. Proven the same session: three questions I escalated to her ([1] a file's provenance, [3] whether a per-id consumer was planned, [6] whether a design was over-engineered) were all already settled — in `in-flight.md`'s frontmatter, in D-390's "ONE RESOLVER, THREE CONSUMERS", and in `feedback_overengineering_boundary_when_future_easier`. Two of them **inverted** an investigative agent's conclusion.

**How to apply:**
- Toolchain question → search the corpus FIRST (decision logs · the north star `DESIGN_SPECS/doc-disciplines/in-code-doc-system-north-star.md` · DESIGN_SPECS · memories · ledgers). Escalate only what the record genuinely does not contain.
- Escalate anyway when it's a **convention/identity** call (a persisted-identifier form, a ledger's tier structure, what a namespace means) — those are hers even on the toolchain plane, because they're policy not algorithm.
- Keep the **integration lens** on by default: how does this land in the dev environment / the toolchain-v1 finish line? She wants that held continuously, not asked about.
- Do NOT dress a readout as a question. If it's analysis, present it as analysis and make the call; a question mark on a finding is still a readout (she names this directly).
- Hot-path/engine architecture is the inverse — there her judgment leads and mine informs (`user_owns_core_design_meet_confidence_genuinely`).
