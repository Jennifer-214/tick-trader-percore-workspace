## 3. The core — D-97 (decision-log entry is authoritative)
Binary FPN can't represent decimals exactly (`2^64/10` ∉ ℤ) — same repr-limit as float; the `~1e-19` parse error is negligible for VALUE (11 orders below the satoshi) but real for exact-decimal EQUALITY/RECONCILIATION. **But FPN ≠ float** — its arithmetic is exact+associative (add/sub) + deterministic (mul) + magnitude-independent-precision; that's the property it buys, not decimal-exactness. The options: **(A)** binary + round-at-reconcile (correct-by-discipline — fragile); **(B)** decimal fixed-point (exact-by-construction — WINS); **(C)** hybrid (binary math + raw-decimal record). Mul/div still round under B, but at a DECIMAL place (= how money is accounted — *more* correct). Notional needs int128 intermediates. Full reasoning: decision-log **D-97** + the conversation.
