# v5.15.5.F.4d.1.E sub-sprint — architecture decisions v2

<!-- D/C/F: D-1 -->
**D-1: Architecture choice — Architecture E++.** Per-node sub-accounts + multi-exchange substrate + headless service + io_uring + WS-API + DPDK (end-game). Phased delivery option D (~6-9 weeks; 8+ sub-ships). Pursuing perfect-architecture path; time-irrelevant; ego-flex motivation accepted.

<!-- D/C/F: D-54 -->
**D-54: O(1) Event-Sourced Aggregator folded into `.E.1`.** Push-model instead of pull-model: each per-node fill triggers atomic_fetch_add on global aggregator's running aggregate (vs aggregator polling all nodes every 100ms). Aggregator's periodic cycle becomes "verify integrity" not "compute totals". Drops kill-switch latency floor from ~100ms to ~50μs (fill arrival time). Improves performance (O(1) vs O(N×cycles)) + correctness (no race window; aggregator state always exact) + accuracy (per-fill bit-exact totals; no cycle-based aggregation drift). Multi-writer atomic on aligned memory; lock-free; tight contention bounded by fill rate (~20/sec at full tilt = negligible). NEW DESIGN_SPEC: `framework-patterns/event-sourced-aggregator-o1-pattern.md` (Stage 3 first canonical at `.E.1`). Refines D-34 (aggregator placement); supersedes Decision D in plan body (cadence-based → event-driven primary; 100ms cycle becomes integrity-verification only).
