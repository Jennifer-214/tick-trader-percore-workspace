- **(A1)** the helper is invoked N times in a for-loop at backtest side (matches signature; signature OK; ShardedBacktestDriver migration becomes ~5 LOC for the loop)
- **(A2)** the helper takes EngineCommon_SlowPathCycleAllCores name (signature changes; semantically what AllCores does today)
- **(A3)** BOTH helpers extracted (OneCore + AllCores wrappers; helper count goes 2 → 3)

Plan body should explicitly choose between A1/A2/A3 before signature lock. (A1) is recommended per cohort+future-easier (each per-core slow thread already calls OneCore; deletion of legacy `centralized` arch per TECH_DEBT-002 simplifies this — only OneCore remains).

But there's ALSO `EngineSharded.hpp` centralized slow-path at `:1840-1953` (gated `if (cfg.engine_arch != ENGINE_ARCH_PER_CORE_SLOW)`) that is THIS SHIP'S responsibility too if helper claims to unify ALL slow-path bodies.
