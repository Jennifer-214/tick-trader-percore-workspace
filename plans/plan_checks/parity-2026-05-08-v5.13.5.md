## Findings — both CRITICAL gaps now CLOSED

### CRITICAL-1 — Use-after-free in train_multi_horizon_worker_fn

- **Original gap:** `MultiHorizonWorkerArgs` extended with
  `snap_label_kind_per_horizon[]` + `snap_training_side` in
  v5.13.5, but worker's snap-capture-before-free block at line
  ~3814 did NOT update to copy them. 4 downstream sites
  dereferenced freed memory (parallel-job populate + serial-mode
  loop call ×2).

- **Fix:** v5.13.5.B (`commit 6f3296c`) — added stack-local copy
  of both fields before `free(args)`; updated 4 downstream reads
  to use stack-local snap_* instead of args->*.

- **Class:** added to RECURRING_BUG_PATTERNS.md as Class 13
  ("Worker-thread struct extended without updating snap-capture-
  before-free block").

### CRITICAL-2 — Single-horizon Train Model handler missing snap

- **Original gap:** Train Model click handler (single-horizon
  path, routed through MultiHorizon worker since v5.11.44)
  populated old snap fields but NOT the v5.13.5 fields. Worker
  read uninitialized memory.

- **Fix:** v5.13.5.A (`commit 743f228`) — added per-iteration
  populate of snap_label_kind_per_horizon[i] + outside-loop
  populate of snap_training_side, mirroring the Train Multi-
  Horizon handler pattern.

- **Class:** flavor 2 of Class 13 (uninitialized snap field at
  click handler vs. use-after-free at worker).
