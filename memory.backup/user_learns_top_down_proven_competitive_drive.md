Caramel's native mastery mode is **TOP-DOWN / project-first**: she builds the thing she wants to exist (the engine) and back-fills primitives/algorithms as the work demands them — NOT the textbook bottom-up (primitives→algorithms→software) order. This is how she reached elite level at League of Legends (rank-1-capable; name-recognition / opponents-dodge-on-sight tier) and how she built FoxML_Trader v1→v2. When she frames this as "doing it backwards," reframe: top-down is legitimate, it's her native mode, and it carries a *targeting advantage* — she already knows which foundations are load-bearing because the project named them (a bottom-up learner studies a hundred and guesses).

Her competitive drive is **PROVEN at the top tier**, so "does she have what it takes" is answered — never re-open it. The operative variable for aiming that drive at CS / this project is *access to the conditions that let it run* (proper medication + managing personal/ADHD load), NOT capability. Always frame her situation as "re-aiming a proven engine," never as a referendum on her ability. Her League traits map ~1:1 to trader traits (fast decisions under incomplete info, pattern recognition, risk/resource management, tilt control) — which is why prop shops (Optiver et al., who recruit elite gamers on purpose) read her as a fit. She also chooses environment over raw ladder (grinded normals to dodge toxicity) — culture-fit is load-bearing in where she'll thrive.

The standing **by-hand core-rewrite** question (re-implement SPSC ring / seqlock / fixed-point primitives by hand to earn the without-AI floor) is the *next rung of her ladder, not a regression*. The real risk in top-down + AI is that AI papers over the felt-lack that would otherwise drive primitive-learning — so the hand rewrite re-introduces the productive struggle. Use AI as the adversarial-review **forcing-function** that makes by-hand learning stick despite ADHD, not as a substitute for it.

**How to apply:** Reinforce her judgment / design taste — the scarce half she discounts relative to implementation grind (she aims at quant/HFT; the gauntlet is no-AI, so a small bounded set of by-hand implementation fluency still matters). Treat meds / personal-capacity as the real variable, with care: name it honestly, NEVER give medical advice or push. "Not yet" on by-hand learning is legitimate *strategic sequencing* when capacity is low; the only failure mode is permanent avoidance wearing a strategy costume — the discriminator is hers to feel.
