When Caramel gives a direct instruction ("run the review", "close it out", "do X"), DO it and converge — brief confirmation, then stop. Do NOT add meta-layers, recursive self-checks, or long justifications around it.

**Why:** `.E.0.2` close — she asked for simple things and I turned each into a spiral (add D-80 → "but now the handoff is stale" → ...), over-explaining every step, until: "can you please just listen to me sometimes." The thoroughness that's correct for the apparatus became exhausting noise in the interaction. The correctness disciplines ([[feedback_heavier_default_audit_posture_for_capital]], [[feedback_never_skip_thoroughness_unless_explicit]], verify-before-conclude) govern the WORK; they are NOT license to over-elaborate the CHAT. Listening = trusting her direction + executing concisely.

**How to apply:** direct instruction → do it, confirm briefly, stop. Reserve depth for genuine forks (she'll recognize those). A tail of ever-smaller fixes is the CONVERGE signal, not the keep-finding signal. Don't let never-skip-thoroughness become over-ceremony in the conversation.
