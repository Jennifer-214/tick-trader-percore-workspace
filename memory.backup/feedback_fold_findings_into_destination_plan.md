When current work (a ship, an audit, an investigation) surfaces a finding or information that BELONGS in a DIFFERENT plan — it sharpens that plan's decision, adds scope, reframes one of its findings, gates something in it, or gives it greater information — AMEND/APPEND the finding INTO that plan (its body + its decision log if it has one), dated, with a back-reference to the source. Do NOT leave it only in the current ship's register/handoff/decision-log.

**Why:** a finding relevant to a future plan, if left only in the current ship's docs, is LOST by the time that plan is worked — compaction degrades, context resets, and the current ship's register is not re-read at the future plan's pickup. Folding it INTO the destination plan means the plan carries current knowledge: its eventual review/work starts from current truth, not the stale version. Canonical instance (2026-06-10): `.E.0.10`'s adversarial torn-read sweep (9 sites, 3 live capital-control, the live-enable gate, the F-2 "free-bonus"→"capital-safety" reframe) folded INTO the `.E.1` plan body (Bug Classes + acceptance) + decision-log `F-2-AMEND`, so `.E.1` starts from the sharpened scope, not the stale "1 KS race / free bonus." Without the fold, `.E.1` would have re-derived (or missed) the systemic scope weeks later.

**How to apply:** (1) TRIGGER — current work surfaces a finding that BELONGS in another plan (sharpens its decision / adds scope / reframes a finding / gates it / gives greater info). (2) ACTION — amend/append it INTO that plan (body + decision log), dated, with a back-ref to the source register/ship. (3) BOUNDARY — this is REACTIVE (fold what current work surfaced), NOT proactive plan-scouting (don't go reading the whole future plan to audit it — that's the separate plan-review pass). Discriminator: did CURRENT work surface it? → fold it. Are you going looking through the future plan? → that's the review, separate. (4) VERIFY — at the destination plan's pickup/review, the folded findings are present (the plan carries current truth). This is the cross-plan extension of the auto-write contracts (finding→ledger becomes finding→destination-plan) + the forward-deposit half of [[feedback_forward_promise_auto_write_verification]]. Mechanical-enforcement candidate: a forward-promise-style check (a ship/register that names another plan as a fold target → verify the fold landed in that plan).
